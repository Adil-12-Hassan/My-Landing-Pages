VOID VOCAL
Real-Time Voice Changer & Pitch Shifter
🎤 What does this do?
VOID VOCAL listens to your microphone live and transforms your voice in real time - shift your pitch up or down, add autotune snap, slap on reverb, crush it with distortion, add robot effects, and more. Think T-Pain, Travis Scott, Daft Punk - all in your browser, no install needed.
  • 1
    Click "Start" below - your browser will ask for microphone permission. Allow it.
  • 2
    Speak or sing into your mic. You'll see your voice visualized as a waveform on screen.
  • 3
    Pick a Preset (like "Travis Scott" or "Robot") or tweak the sliders to shape your sound.
  • 4
    Wear headphones to hear the processed output without feedback echo.
⚠️ Headphones recommended · Works best in Chrome / Edge · No data is sent anywhere

About VOID VOCAL

VOID VOCAL is a real-time voice changer and pitch shifter that runs entirely in your browser - no app, no plugin, no server. It listens to your microphone live and applies a professional-grade DSP (Digital Signal Processing) chain to your voice: pitch shifting, autotune snap, reverb, distortion, bit crushing, vibrato, harmony, delay, and more. Think T-Pain, Travis Scott, Daft Punk - all in a single HTML file.
